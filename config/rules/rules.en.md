## 1. The goal

2 ways to win:

- Capture the enemy keep with at least one surviving soldier to win.
- Otherwise, after {{rounds}} turns each, the highest score wins.

## 2. Set up the board

- Board: {{size}} × {{size}} spaces.
- Starting tile with a keep in the middle of each side.
- Take turns placing {{openingCount}} tile(s) each on empty spaces.
- 2 workers each.

## 3. Take your turn

On each turn, you can use your workers and soldiers.
Units can move {{workerMoves}} spaces each turn.
Each worker can perform one action, before or after moving:

- Explore
- Build
- Produce

## 4. Explore

Explore: place a terrain tile next to a worker.
You can also reveal a single space of any terrain you choose for {{singleTerrainCost}} gold.
Replacing the available terrain tiles costs {{rerollCost}} gold, once per turn.

## 5. Build

Build: pay the cost and place a building tile on the worker’s space or next to it.
Match the terrain shown in the pattern. The building must be placed on an empty space.

The market starts with 3 building cards:

- After building, cards above the purchased card move down to fill its space. A new card enters at the top.
- After all players have taken a turn, add a card at the top and move the others down.
- The market holds at most 5 cards. When a 6th card enters, discard the bottom card.

Special buildings:

- Bridges and tunnels let you cross water and mountains, respectively.
- Clearing lets you turn a forest into a plain and gain wood.
- A house gives you a new worker, active from your next turn.
- Barracks let you produce one unit per turn in exchange for wheat.

## 6. Produce and progress

Producing lets you gain resources.
You can gain resources from:

- Empty plain: 1 wheat.
- Empty forest: 1 wood.
- Your building: its listed production. Each building produces only once per turn. Only resource-producing buildings need a worker.

Replacing the available cards costs {{rerollCost}} gold, once per turn.

## 7. Fight and conquer

Each soldier can move and attack. A soldier costs 2 wheat. You can:

- Group your soldiers by stacking pieces.
- Split your army by unstacking pieces.

Each soldier provides 1 strength. An army has 1 bonus strength when defending. The stronger side keeps as many soldiers as the difference in strength.

- If the attacking army survives a battle, it captures the attacked space.
- A captured worker returns to the keep, active on your next turn.

Towers and keeps have 2 strength. You therefore need an army of 3 soldiers to defeat them.

Workers can capture a flag or take ownership of an undefended enemy building.

## 8. Optional expansion: Missions

Missions are optional. These 3 objectives appear at the top of the board and reward the first player to meet the requirement.

Once completed, they are replaced by a mission from the next level.

If a new mission appears and you already meet its requirement, you must still contribute to it during that turn to complete it. For example, for a “Produce 10 wood” mission, if you already have 11, you must produce a 12th to complete it.
